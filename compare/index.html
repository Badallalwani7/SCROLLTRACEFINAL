Tracker, blocker, or pause?

ScrollTrace vs Shortstop vs one sec vs ScreenZen

By Badal Lalwani, developer of ScrollTrace · Updated 11 October 2026 · I make ScrollTrace, so read this with that in mind.

ScrollTrace measures short-form scrolling: it counts the Reels, Shorts and TikToks you swipe through. Shortstop blocks those feeds. one sec and ScreenZen add a pause before you open an app. Pick a tracker if you don't yet know how much you scroll, a blocker if you want it gone, and a pause app if you want to keep access but slow the habit down.

Three kinds of doomscrolling app

Tracker

Shows the number

Counts what you actually scroll. Doesn't stop you. Example: ScrollTrace.

Blocker

Removes the feed

Closes Reels or Shorts when you open them. Example: Shortstop.

Pause / friction

Slows the open

Makes you wait or breathe before an app opens. Examples: one sec, ScreenZen.

ScrollTrace vs Shortstop

Both are Android apps built around Reels, Shorts and TikTok, and both use the Accessibility service. The difference is what happens when you swipe. Shortstop closes the feed so you can't scroll it. ScrollTrace lets you scroll and records how many videos you went through. If you already know you want Shorts gone, Shortstop does that directly. If you want to see your baseline first, or you want to cut down rather than cut off, ScrollTrace gives you the number.

ScrollTrace vs one sec

one sec interrupts the moment you open an app, with a breathing exercise or a short delay, and is backed by peer-reviewed research on reducing app openings. It works on the decision to open. ScrollTrace works on what happens after you are already in the feed. one sec is also available on iPhone; ScrollTrace is not.

ScrollTrace vs ScreenZen

ScreenZen is a donation-supported screen time app that adds wait times, open limits and scheduled blocks across many apps and websites. It is broader than ScrollTrace. ScrollTrace is narrower: it only looks at short-form video, and reports it in videos rather than minutes of app time.

ScrollTrace vs Android Digital Wellbeing

Digital Wellbeing, built into Android, shows how long each app was open. It cannot separate Reels from DMs inside Instagram, or Shorts from long videos inside YouTube. ScrollTrace counts only the short-video part.

Using two together

A tracker and a blocker don't conflict in purpose. A common approach: run ScrollTrace for a week to see your real count and the hours when it spikes, then use a blocker or a pause app for those hours, and keep ScrollTrace running to check that the number falls. Two Accessibility-based apps can occasionally interfere with each other; if counts look wrong, test with one at a time.

How to choose

  • "I don't know how bad it is." Start with a tracker.
  • "I know, and I want Reels gone." Use a blocker.
  • "I open Instagram without thinking." Use a pause app.
  • "I'm on iPhone." ScrollTrace and Shortstop are Android only; look at one sec or ScreenZen.
