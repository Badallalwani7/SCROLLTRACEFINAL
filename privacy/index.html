Privacy and permissions.

By Badal Lalwani · Updated 11 October 2026 · Applies to ScrollTrace 2.5.0 for Android

ScrollTrace uses Android's Accessibility service only inside Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, and only to detect that you swiped to a new short video. It does not read captions, comments, usernames or messages. Your scroll history is stored on your phone. A small amount of usage data goes to Google Firebase, and account and purchase data goes to Google and the subscription provider. You can export or delete your history at any time.

What the Accessibility permission can see

The service is configured to receive events only from Instagram, YouTube and TikTok (including TikTok Lite). From those apps it uses:

  • Scroll events — that a scroll happened, and the position in the list.
  • Which screen is open — the internal name of the container, such as the Reels or Shorts player, to decide whether to count.

It does not read or store post text, captions, comments, usernames, profile details, messages or the videos themselves. It does not take screenshots, and it does not tap, type or act inside other apps.

Stored on your phone

  • A count of videos per app, per day, with times — the history behind your charts
  • Session records (when a scrolling session started and ended)
  • Your settings, such as daily limits

This history lives in a local database inside ScrollTrace. It is not uploaded to a ScrollTrace server.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Accessibility service — detect swipes in Reels, Shorts and TikTok, as above.
  • Notifications — limit alerts and the tracking notification.
  • Foreground service and start at boot — keep counting when the app is closed and after a restart.
  • Internet — sign-in, purchases and analytics.
  • Google Play Billing — optional Pro subscription.

Export and delete

  • Export: Settings → Privacy & Data → Export data as CSV.
  • Delete history: Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete all data. This permanently wipes your scroll history from the phone.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes the local database.
  • Account and analytics data: to ask for deletion of your account or associated analytics records, use the contact form.

Turning tracking off

You can switch off tracking for Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts in ScrollTrace settings, or turn off the ScrollTrace Accessibility service entirely in Android Settings → Accessibility. Nothing is counted while it is off.

Children

ScrollTrace is not directed at children under 13.
