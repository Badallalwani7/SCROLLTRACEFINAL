06 / Questions

Asked and answered.

Q.01What is ScrollTrace?

ScrollTrace is a free Android app that counts how many Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok videos you swipe through each day, and estimates how much time that took. It shows when and where you doomscroll so you can decide what to change. It is made by Badal Lalwani, an independent developer in Jabalpur, India.

Q.02Does ScrollTrace block Reels or Shorts?

No. ScrollTrace measures short-form scrolling; it does not block or hide feeds. You can set a daily limit for Reels and Shorts and get an alert when you pass it, but the apps keep working. If you want a hard block, use a blocker app alongside it.

Q.03Which apps does ScrollTrace track?

Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok (including TikTok Lite). It only counts swipes in the short-video player. Regular Instagram feed, Stories, YouTube long videos and messages are not counted.

Q.04How does ScrollTrace calculate time spent?

Each swipe to a new video counts as one video. Time is estimated by multiplying the count by a typical video length: about 22 seconds per Instagram Reel, 35 seconds per YouTube Short and 20 seconds per TikTok. It is an estimate, not a stopwatch.

Q.05Is ScrollTrace free?

Yes. ScrollTrace is free to download and tracking is free. An optional Pro subscription adds deeper analytics, reality-check cards, pattern analysis and custom alerts. Current prices are shown in Google Play.

Q.06Does ScrollTrace work on iPhone?

No. ScrollTrace is Android only and needs Android 8.0 or later. iOS does not allow apps to observe scrolling in other apps.

Q.07Why does ScrollTrace need the Accessibility permission?

Android's Accessibility Service is the only way an app can notice that you swiped to the next video inside Instagram, YouTube or TikTok. ScrollTrace limits the service to those apps and uses it only to detect scroll events and whether you are in Reels, Shorts or TikTok. It does not read post text, comments, usernames or messages.

Q.08Does my scroll data leave my phone?

Your scroll history is stored in a database on your phone. ScrollTrace also sends limited usage analytics to Google Firebase (for example, that a Reel was scrolled, with no content) and uses Google sign-in and a subscription provider for accounts and payments. Full details are on the privacy page.

Q.09Can I delete my data?

Yes. In Settings → Privacy & Data, choose Delete all data to wipe your scroll history from the phone. You can also export everything as a CSV file first.
